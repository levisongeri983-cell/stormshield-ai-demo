This dashboard simulates the real Python prototype's logic: threshold-based risk scoring on river discharge trends, ranked by the hazard-agnostic Watershed Kernel, routed to specific responder organizations, and made explainable through Trace. The actual codebase includes a working flood module, a real GloFAS live-data integration, a stub wildfire module proving a second hazard plugs into the kernel with zero changes, and 19 passing automated tests.
